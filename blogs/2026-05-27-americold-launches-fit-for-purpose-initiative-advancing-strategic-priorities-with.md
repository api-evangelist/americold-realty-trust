Americold launched the "Fit for Purpose" initiative on May 27, 2026, targeting over $25 million in annual overhead savings by Q1 2027. CEO Rob Chambers said the program aims to drive clearer accountability, faster execution, and stronger performance by streamlining the company's overhead structure.
