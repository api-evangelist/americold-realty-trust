Americold opened a new temperature-controlled facility at Port Saint John in New Brunswick, Canada, developed with DP World and CPKC to integrate cold storage with maritime and rail access. The facility offers approximately 22,000 pallet positions and is expected to support up to 100 jobs while streamlining perishable goods movement between Eastern Canada and international markets.
